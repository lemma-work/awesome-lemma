# Wiring a PSU-150

Wire it with the power off at the board.

### 1. Isolate the circuit
Who: Installer
Turn off the circuit at the board and check it's dead with a tester.

### 2. Connect the mains side
Who: Installer
Live to L, neutral to N. The PSU-150 has no earth terminal; it's double insulated.

### 3. Connect the strip
Who: Installer
Red to V+, black to V−. Keep the run under 5 m from one end.

### 4. Fit the cover
Who: Installer
Clip the terminal cover on until both sides click.

### 5. Test
Who: Installer
Power on. The strip should reach full brightness within a second.
