# Choosing a driver

Add up the watts of every strip on the driver, then add 20 % headroom.

- **PSU-60**: up to 48 W of strip.
- **PSU-150**: up to 120 W.
- **PSU-300**: up to 240 W.

For a 10 W/m strip, a PSU-150 runs 12 m. Over 5 m in one run, feed the strip from both ends to avoid a dimmer far end.
