# Lead times

Standard profiles in 2 m and 3 m lengths ship in **3 working days** from Leeds.

- Cut-to-length profiles: **5 working days**.
- Orders over 200 m: **8 working days**; ask for a date before you promise one.
- Drivers and accessories ship the next working day when ordered by 2 pm.

Bank holidays add a day. If a customer needs it sooner, ask the warehouse before you say yes.
