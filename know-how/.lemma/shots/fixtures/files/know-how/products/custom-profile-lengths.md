# Custom profile lengths

We cut any profile to length, from **150 mm to 3,000 mm**, in 1 mm steps.

- Cutting is free on orders of 10 lengths or more; under that it's £2.50 a cut.
- Tolerance is ±1 mm.
- End caps are fitted on request; say "capped" on the order.

Cut lengths can't be returned unless they're faulty.
