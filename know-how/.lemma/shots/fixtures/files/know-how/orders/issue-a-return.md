# Issue a return

Returns are accepted within **30 days** of delivery, unused and in the box.

### 1. Find the order
Who: Customer service
Look up the order by number or the customer's email. Check it was delivered less than 30 days ago.

### 2. Check the item is returnable
Who: Customer service
Unused and in its box. Cut lengths come back only if they're faulty.

### 3. Raise the return
Who: Customer service
In the order, choose Return, pick the lines and the reason.

### 4. Send the label
Who: Customer service
The return label is emailed to the customer from the order. Tell them to drop it at any parcel shop.

### 5. Receive it
Who: Warehouse
When it arrives, check it against the return and mark it received.

### 6. Inspect it
Who: Warehouse
Unused and complete: mark it restockable. Damaged: photograph it and flag it to customer service.

### 7. Refund
Who: Customer service
Refund to the original payment within 5 working days of receiving it. Tell the customer it's done.
